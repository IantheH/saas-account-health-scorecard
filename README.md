# SaaS Account Health Scorecard

A platform-agnostic account health scorecard for Customer Success teams, Technical Account Managers, and SaaS operators.

This framework helps track customer health across adoption, engagement, support activity, stakeholder alignment, and retention risk.

---

## Purpose

Customer health is more than product usage.

A customer may log in often but still be at risk if stakeholders are disengaged, business goals are unclear, support issues are recurring, or the customer does not understand the value they are receiving.

This scorecard helps organize account health signals so teams can identify risks earlier, prioritize outreach, and support stronger customer outcomes.

## Account Health Categories

This framework reviews account health across five core areas:

### 1. Product Adoption

Measures whether the customer is using the product in ways that support their goals.

Signals may include:

- active usage
- feature adoption
- workflow completion
- user engagement
- repeat usage patterns
- adoption of key use cases

### 2. Stakeholder Engagement

Measures whether the right people are involved and responsive.

Signals may include:

- primary contact engagement
- executive or decision-maker involvement
- meeting attendance
- responsiveness
- internal champion strength
- stakeholder alignment

### 3. Support & Issue Trends

Measures whether support needs are healthy, manageable, or signaling friction.

Signals may include:

- open issues
- repeat questions
- unresolved blockers
- escalation frequency
- support response needs
- technical friction

### 4. Business Goal Alignment

Measures whether the product is connected to meaningful business outcomes.

Signals may include:

- clear success goals
- documented use cases
- progress toward milestones
- measurable outcomes
- customer understanding of value
- alignment between product usage and business priorities

### 5. Retention Risk

Measures the likelihood that the customer may churn, downgrade, disengage, or fail to renew.

Signals may include:

- low usage
- missed meetings
- unclear ownership
- unresolved issues
- budget concerns
- leadership changes
- lack of visible value

---

## Health Ratings

Use a simple Green / Yellow / Red model.

### Green

Customer is engaged, using the platform, seeing value, and moving toward goals.

### Yellow

Customer has some risk signals, incomplete adoption, unclear ownership, or unresolved blockers.

### Red

Customer has major risk signals such as low usage, poor engagement, unresolved issues, unclear value, or cancellation/non-renewal concerns.

---

## How This Helps Customer Success

A strong account health process helps teams:

- prioritize customer outreach
- identify risks before renewal
- support product adoption
- improve stakeholder alignment
- document customer goals
- reduce avoidable churn
- prepare for QBRs and account reviews
- create proactive save plans

---

## Customer Success Principle

Healthy accounts are not created by usage alone.

They are created when customers understand the value of the product, use it consistently, trust the relationship, and can connect the platform to their business goals.
