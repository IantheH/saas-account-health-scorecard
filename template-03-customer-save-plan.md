# Customer Save Plan

Use this template when a customer is at risk of churn, downgrade, cancellation, disengagement, or non-renewal.

The goal is to understand the root cause, rebuild trust, reconnect the product to business outcomes, and define a clear path forward.

- What value have they received so far?
- Where has the experience fallen short?
- Is the issue related to adoption, support, product fit, communication, or expectations?
- What would need to change for the customer to feel confident continuing?
